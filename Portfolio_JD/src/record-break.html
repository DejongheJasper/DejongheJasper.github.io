Record Break

For the fictional brand Record Break, I collaborated with Matthias Van de Voorde to design and animate a page loader animation. Using Adobe After Effects, we each created individual animations and then merged them into one smooth, cohesive sequence. This project allowed me to explore motion design and strengthen my skills in animation workflows.
